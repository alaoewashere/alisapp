## 🎯 TIPS for best Higgsfield results

- **Upload real assets** every time: the app icon (`assets/souqak_app_icon.png`), a real screenshot, and the logo. The AI matches your actual brand better.
- **Keep the two non-negotiables in every prompt:** `volt green #D4FF3A` + `black/near-black background`. That's the brand.
- **Always specify aspect ratio:** `9:16` for reels/TikTok, `1:1` or `4:5` for feed posts.
- **For UGC**, add words like *"authentic, filmed on a phone, natural lighting, real person, not polished"* — that's what makes it look UGC, not corporate.
- **Generate variations:** run the same prompt 3–4 times and pick the best; change only the hook line to A/B test.
- **Hooks to rotate** (first 3 seconds decide everything):
  - "لو بعت شي بالعراق على واتساب... تعرف القصة 😮‍💨"
  - "كل العراق صار بسوق واحد 🇮🇶"
  - "ليش تبيع بمجموعات وي حسة و انت تكدر بنقرة وحدة؟"
  - "محاولة بيع موبايلك بالعراق 😭"
