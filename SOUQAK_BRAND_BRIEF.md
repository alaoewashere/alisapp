# SOUQAK — Brand & Marketing Brief

## 1. THE ELEVATOR PITCH

**SOUQAK (سوقك) is Iraq's smart classifieds marketplace** — a mobile app where anyone in Iraq can **buy, sell, and connect** in seconds. Post a listing with photos, browse what's near you on a live map, chat directly with sellers, and get smart alerts when something you want appears.

Think **"sahibinden.com / OLX, but built for Iraq"** — Arabic-first, local-first, and designed clean like a modern fintech app, not a cluttered ad board.

**One line:** *"سوقك — بيع، اشتري، وتواصل. كل العراق بسوق واحد."*
**One line (EN):** *"SOUQAK — Buy, sell, and connect. All of Iraq in one marketplace."*

## 4. KEY FEATURES (the "what it includes" list)

**Core**
- 📝 **Post in seconds** — photos, price, category, location, description
- 🔍 **Smart search & filters** — by category, city, price, condition
- 🗺️ **Live map view** — see listings near you, clustered map pins
- 💬 **Direct chat** — 1-to-1 buyer ↔ seller messaging per listing (real-time)
- ❤️ **Favorites** — save listings to come back to
- 🔔 **Smart alerts** — get notified when a matching listing is posted

**Trust & Safety**
- ✅ **Seller verification** — verified badge for trusted sellers
- 🛡️ **Admin moderation** — every listing reviewed before going live
- 🚫 **Blocked words & report system** — keeps the marketplace clean
- ⭐ **Ratings** — buyers & sellers rate each other

**Account**
- 📲 **Phone OTP login** (+964) + Sign in with Apple
- 👤 Profiles, listing history, guest browsing (no login needed to look)

**Listing Tiers** (monetization)
- **عادي (Standard)** — basic listing, 30 days
- **برو (Pro)** — higher visibility, verified badge, weekly auto-bump, more photos *(coming soon)*
- **مميز (Premium)** — top ranking, daily auto-bump, buyer alerts, analytics *(coming soon)*

## 5. BRAND COLORS  🎨

The app uses a **dark "fintech" theme** — premium, modern, high-contrast. NOT a typical bright classifieds look.

### Primary palette

| Role | Name | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Accent / Brand** | **Volt Green** | `#D4FF3A` | Logo, buttons, active states, links, highlights |
| **Background** | Deep Canvas | `#131315` | Page background (near-black) |
| **Surface** | Field Carbon | `#18181A` | Cards, inputs, sheets |
| **Text** | Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Primary text & icons |
| **Muted text** | White 60% | `rgba(255,255,255,0.6)` | Secondary text |

> **The signature color is Volt Green `#D4FF3A` on near-black.** That lime-on-black is the brand. Always pair them.
> *(Marketing assets sometimes use `#D4F54A` — a near-identical lime. The exact app token is `#D4FF3A`.)*

### Accent / tier colors

| Role | HEX |
|---|---|
| Premium Gold | `#F5A623` |
| Pro Green (badge) | `#999F54` |
| Pending (orange) | `#FF9800` |
| Approved (green) | `#4CAF50` |
| Rejected (red) | `#F44336` |
| Heart / favorite | `#E63946` |

### Gradients
- **Primary CTA:** Volt → `#B6E62E` (top-left → bottom-right)
- **Premium badge:** `#F5D77A` → `#C9921E` (gold sheen)

## 9. MARKETING / VIDEO BRIEF  🎬

### Visual rules for any video or ad
- **Background:** pure black / near-black (`#000` – `#131315`)
- **Hero color:** Volt Green `#D4FF3A` for text pops, the logo, CTAs
- **Mood:** sleek, fast, modern — fintech energy, not flea-market
- **Always end on:** logo + "سوقك" + "قريباً / حمّل الآن" + App Store badge

### Hooks (first 3 seconds — pick one)
- *"لو بعت شي بالعراق على واتساب... تعرف القصة 😮‍💨"*
- *"ليش تبيع بمجموعات وي حسة و انت تكدر بنقرة وحدة؟"*
- *"كل العراق صار بسوق واحد 🇮🇶"*

### 30-second TikTok/Reel structure
| Time | Scene | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | **Hook** | Black screen, text appears word-by-word — relatable selling pain |
| 0:03–0:10 | **Problem** | WhatsApp/Facebook chaos — too many messages, no sales |
| 0:10–0:22 | **Solution** | Show SOUQAK: map view, chat, smart alerts (2–3s each, real screen recordings) |
| 0:22–0:30 | **Reveal** | Logo grows from center, green glow, "سوقك جاي قريب" + follow/download CTA |

> Music: calm intro that **drops/explodes** the moment "سوقك" appears. Use a trending Iraqi beat or a trending motion sound.

### Feature points to highlight in any ad
🗺️ خريطة حية · 💬 دردشة مباشرة · 🔔 تنبيه ذكي · ✅ بائعون موثقون · 🆓 تصفح مجاني
